// FAIR PLAY & ELIGIBILITY

Who Can Participate & Rules

Clear, simple guidelines so every team competes on an equal playing field.

01

Students Only

Open to all undergraduate, postgraduate, and diploma students from any recognized college or university. Valid student IDs will be verified.

02

Exactly 5 Members

Every team must consist of exactly 5 students. Cross-college and inter-departmental teams are 100% allowed and encouraged.

03

One Registration

A participant can only be registered under one squad. Multi-team entries by the same individual will lead to team disqualification.

04

₹750 Entry Fee

A flat non-refundable fee of ₹750 covers the entire squad of 5 (only ₹150 per person). Entry is confirmed once payment screenshot is validated.

05

AI Tools Allowed 🤖

Participants are completely free to use AI tools (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Cursor, v0, Bolt, Copilot, etc.) to design, code, and generate websites. Speed, innovation, and functional execution are what win!

06

Organizer Decision Final

Evaluation rubrics, bracket matchups, and referee verdicts are conclusive. Respectful communication in official groups is mandatory.
